The Little Lantern

Once upon a time a small lantern lived on a shelf by the window.
Every night it watched the stars and wished that it could shine
as brightly as they did.

One evening a child took the lantern down from the shelf.
She carried it into the dark garden and lit the tiny flame.
The lantern glowed, and the whole garden seemed to smile.

From that night on the little lantern was happy.
It had learned that even a small light can make the darkness kind.
